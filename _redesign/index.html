Nishad Singhi

Hey — I'm Nishad. I'm an ELLIS PhD student working with Anna Rohrbach and Marcus Rohrbach, and I'm collaborating on OpenThoughts-Agent under Ludwig Schmidt.

Right now I'm interning at Apple MLR with Alex Toshev and Andrew Szot.

My research lives at the intersection of embodied AI, agents, reasoning, and multimodality.

Before this, I spent time at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and UCLA, and was lucky to work with Kai-Wei Chang, Aditya Grover, Wieland Brendel, Zeynep Akata, and Falk Lieder. My work has picked up a Best Paper Award at an ICLR 2023 workshop and the Diversity and Inclusion Award at CogSci 2023.

Earlier still: undergrad at IIT Delhi with Sumeet Agarwal, Tapan Gandhi, and Sumitava Mukherjee.

Off-hours: singing & the guitar, badminton, photography, and an unreasonable amount of masala dosa.

I'm always up to meet new people — say hi.
