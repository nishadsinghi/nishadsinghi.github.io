Nishad Singhi

PhD student · ELLIS · currently @ Apple MLR

I am an ELLIS PhD student working with Anna Rohrbach and Marcus Rohrbach. I am also collaborating on OpenThoughts-Agent under Ludwig Schmidt. Currently I am doing an internship at Apple MLR with Alex Toshev and Andrew Szot.

My research focuses on Embodied AI, Agents, Reasoning, and Multimodality.

Previously I spent time at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and UCLA. I had the fortune of working with Kai-Wei Chang, Aditya Grover, Wieland Brendel, Zeynep Akata, and Falk Lieder. Before that I was an undergraduate at IIT Delhi with Sumeet Agarwal, Tapan Gandhi, and Sumitava Mukherjee.

Awards: Best Paper Award at the RTML workshop at ICLR 2023; Diversity and Inclusion Award at CogSci 2023.
